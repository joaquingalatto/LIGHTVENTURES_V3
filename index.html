02

Founder Fit

03

Technicalities

Business model, market fit / timing, execution frameworks, organizational design, and discipline matter. We align, leverage these concepts, and achieve success together.

Where in the lifecycle we participate

We emphasize investments in earlier stages, but participate in all stages of the company lifecycle. We have personally navigated all stages of the start-to-exit journey, and believe that our participation throughout the continuum adds valuable perspective to our portfolio companies — from what to prepare for ahead, or what to adopt faster as you scale.

01

Early Stage

At the early stage, founders are most focused on building the founding team, developing a thoughtful product roadmap, and identifying initial customers in order to determine early market validation and product revenues.

02

Growth Stage

As portfolio companies start to scale, we assist with team building, identifying additional customers, product expansion opportunities, and introductions to potential new co-investors.

03

Late Stage

Our focus turns toward broader strategic opportunities that are essential to building an enduring and valuable business, including product line extensions, new forms of capital, and strategic transactions that accelerate growth.

04

Exit / Liquidity

Decisions taken at this stage have an outsized impact on the eventual narrative. Thoughtful engagement with management teams helps ensure the optimal outcome is achieved and the relationship continues well beyond one company.

Incubator

When the companies and ideas we want to back don’t exist, we help build them.

Earliest Stage

Our incubator model is built on a simple conviction: the earliest stage is where the most value is created, and where experienced operators can make the greatest difference. We co-develop ideas, help recruit founding teams, and work alongside entrepreneurs through the messy, non-linear process of finding product-market fit.

University Pipeline

We engage the entrepreneurial ecosystem at its source, partnering with leading universities to identify exceptional founders early, before they have a pitch deck, and sometimes before they have a company. We mentor promising teams, stress-test ideas, and provide hands-on support in the moments that matter most.

IP and Moats

Throughout the process we leverage deep intellectual property expertise from tested practitioners on our team to validate, build, and protect business moats in order to maximize company advantages and enterprise value.
